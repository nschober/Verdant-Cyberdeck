# Authorized use

Use Verdant Cyberdeck only on equipment and networks you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Establish scope, permitted techniques, timing, contacts and data-handling requirements before an engagement. Shared radio environments and third-party services may introduce constraints beyond ownership of a test device.

Dashboard startup is passive. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth discovery require an explicit selection. Pwnagotchi Start/Restart controls operate the existing service, whose own dependencies may initiate active wireless operations; a confirmation screen describes that consequence. Tool launchers do not establish authorization or safe target scope for you.

Do not use the project to obtain unauthorized access, capture third-party credentials or communications, disrupt networks, evade an agreed scope, or violate applicable laws or contractual restrictions. Stop testing when authorization expires or unexpected third-party impact occurs.

Keep captures, credentials, handshakes, learned data and client identifiers out of source control. Minimize collection, protect retained data, and remove it according to the engagement's retention terms. The repository's ignore rules are a convenience, not a substitute for reviewing every commit and archive.
